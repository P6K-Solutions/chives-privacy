Chives Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · P6K Solutions

The short version: Chives does not collect, send, or share any personal information. The meals and weekly plans you create are saved only on your device.

Who we are

Chives is a meal planning app made by P6K Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains how Chives handles information when you use the app on Android or iOS.

Information the app stores

To work, Chives saves the following on your device:

  • The meals you add to your rotation (meal names and categories)
  • Your weekly meal plan, including any notes you add to a day and which days you have locked

This information stays in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

None. Chives does not:

  • require an account or ask for your name, email address, or other contact details
  • use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools
  • access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files
  • send any data over the internet

Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not sell, rent, or share personal information with anyone.

App stores

Chives is distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. Those stores may collect information about downloads, purchases, and app performance (for example, crash reports, if you have chosen to share them with the store) under their own privacy policies. We do not control that collection. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy for details.

Deleting your data

You can remove meals from your rotation at any time inside the app. To delete everything Chives has stored, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your device settings. Because nothing is stored on our side, there is no data for us to delete.

Children's privacy

Chives does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Security

Your meal data is kept in the app's private storage, which your device's operating system protects from other apps.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Chives handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version starts collecting any information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
